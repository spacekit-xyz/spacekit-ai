# Crypto Scams Information

A collection of a strong set of **other scam categories** that are highly active in crypto, adjacent to crypto, or sentiment‑rich enough to stress‑test a classifier. I'll keep everything high‑level and safe, but specific enough for dataset design.

---

# 🧩 **Major Scam Types Beyond Pig‑Butchering, Romance, and Sextortion**

Below is a structured breakdown you can use to generate **sentiment prompts**, **edge cases**, and **ambiguous tones** for your model.

---

## 🏦 **1. Impersonation & Authority Scams**
These are gold for sentiment because they often mix urgency, fear, and confusion.

- **Bank impersonation** (“Your account is locked, verify now”)  
- **Government impersonation** (IRS, Social Security, tax refund threats)  
- **Tech support impersonation** (fake Microsoft/Apple support)  
- **CEO / executive impersonation** (“urgent wire transfer”)  

**Sentiment angles:** fear, urgency, confusion, mild suspicion, frustration.

---

## 💸 **2. Investment & Trading Scams (non–pig butchering)**
- **Fake crypto airdrops**  
- **Pump‑and‑dump groups**  
- **Fake trading bots / signal groups**  
- **“Guaranteed returns” wealth managers**  
- **NFT rug pulls**  

**Sentiment angles:** greed, hope, cautious optimism, betrayal, anger.

---

## 📱 **3. Account Takeover & Credential Harvesting**
- **Phishing emails/SMS**  
- **SIM‑swap setups**  
- **Fake login portals**  
- **QR‑code phishing**  

**Sentiment angles:** panic, regret, annoyance, relief (if caught early).

---

## 🛍️ **4. Marketplace & Payment Platform Scams**
- **Fake buyers** (overpayment, refund scams)  
- **Fake sellers** (never ship goods)  
- **Chargeback abuse**  
- **“Accidental payment” scams**  

**Sentiment angles:** irritation, suspicion, mixed (“the deal looked good but…”).

---

## 🧾 **5. Subscription & Billing Scams**
- **Dark patterns** (hard‑to‑cancel trials)  
- **Fake invoice scams**  
- **Unauthorized recurring charges**  

**Sentiment angles:** annoyance, resignation, mild anger.

---

## 🧪 **6. Verification & Recovery Scams**
- **Fake “account recovery” helpers**  
- **Fake crypto wallet recovery services**  
- **Fake customer support chats**  

**Sentiment angles:** desperation, cautious hope, later betrayal.

---

## 🛠️ **7. Job & Income Scams**
- **Fake remote job offers**  
- **Check‑cashing scams**  
- **“Reshipping” mule recruitment**  
- **Fake recruiter impersonation**  

**Sentiment angles:** optimism → confusion → fear.

---

## 🎁 **8. Prize, Lottery, and Giveaway Scams**
- **“You won a prize, pay fees to claim”**  
- **Fake sweepstakes**  
- **Social media giveaway impersonation**  

**Sentiment angles:** excitement, disbelief, later anger.

---

## 🧳 **9. Travel & Rental Scams**
- **Fake Airbnb listings**  
- **Fake vacation packages**  
- **Fake moving company deposits**  

**Sentiment angles:** disappointment, frustration, mixed (“looked legit but…”).

---

## 🧬 **10. AI‑Assisted Scams (emerging)**
These are increasingly relevant for modern sentiment datasets.

- **Voice cloning for “family emergency” scams**  
- **Deepfake video impersonation**  
- **AI‑generated fake customer support chats**  
- **AI‑generated investment newsletters**  

**Sentiment angles:** disbelief, fear, confusion, shock.

---

# 🧨 **11. Business Email Compromise (BEC) Variants**
- **Invoice redirection**  
- **Vendor impersonation**  
- **Payroll rerouting**  

**Sentiment angles:** operational panic, blame, urgency.

---

# 🧿 **12. Charity & Disaster Relief Scams**
- **Fake donation drives**  
- **Fake GoFundMe pages**  

**Sentiment angles:** empathy, guilt, betrayal.

---

# 🧩 **13. Crypto‑Specific Scams (beyond investment fraud)**
- **Fake wallet apps**  
- **Dusting attacks** — usually a **privacy / tracing** nuisance or precursor to follow‑on scams; sentiment is often **confusion or mild annoyance**, not the same anger profile as a rug or total wallet drain.  
- **Fake staking platforms**  
- **Malicious smart contracts**  

**Sentiment angles:** technical confusion, regret, anger.
